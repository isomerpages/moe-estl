<p>We were founded as an experimental engineering team in July 2014 by then-Minister
for Education Heng Swee Keat and NUS Associate Professor of Computing Science
Dr. Ben Leong to push digital transformation in education.</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">

<p>From a small team of engineers, we have evolved into a product team comprising
Software Engineers, UX Designers, Agile Delivery Managers and Education
Officers.&nbsp;</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="ESTL group photo" src="/images/aboutus/about_story02.jpg">

<p>Today, ESTL is part of the Information Technology Division of the Ministry
of Education and Government Technology Agency.&nbsp;</p>
